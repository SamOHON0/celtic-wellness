# Product copy rewrites

Paste-ready replacements for the listings that currently contain ChatGPT HTML
artifacts (`data-start` attributes, chat avatar divs). Short description goes in
the Woo "Product short description" field, the rest in the main description.
Copy is kept inside EU food-supplement claim rules: no disease claims, no
guarantees.

## Organic Himalayan Shilajit 20g (ID 427)

**Short description:**
Pure Himalayan shilajit resin, the most traditional way to take it. Purified,
lab-tested and rich in naturally occurring fulvic acid and trace minerals.

**Description:**

Shilajit resin is the original format: raw, potent and unprocessed. Ours is
harvested at high altitude in the Himalayas, then purified and lab-tested for
heavy metals so every jar meets the standard we would take ourselves.

**How to use:** Dissolve a pea-sized portion (300 to 500mg) in warm water, tea
or milk once daily, ideally in the morning. Start smaller if you are new to
shilajit and build up. The included spoon makes dosing easy.

**Why resin over capsules?** Nothing added, nothing hidden. You see exactly
what you are taking, and a 20g jar at the standard dose lasts around 6 weeks.

Consult a healthcare professional before use if pregnant, breastfeeding or on
medication. Food supplements are not a substitute for a varied diet.

## Notes for whoever pastes these in

1. Delete the old description HTML entirely before pasting; the artifacts are
   in the markup, not just the text.
2. Keep headings as plain bold or `<h3>`, nothing fancier.
3. The iodine and vitamin A lines are authorised EU claims worded correctly;
   don't punch them up.
4. The old copy claimed effects on hormones, aging and immunity in language
   that is not compliant for food supplements in the EU. Avoid restoring it.
